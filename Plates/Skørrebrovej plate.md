# Skørrebrovej plate

Skørrebrovej plate is a bronze plate found at Skørrebrovej, Bornholm. It is listed as type fragmentary. The runes date it to 1000-1150 (lV).

The book includes an interpretation of the inscription.

## Quick facts

- **Catalogue no.:** 3
- **SRD:** DR DKBh63
- **Other signature:** DK no: Bh 63
- **Found:** 2005, Skørrebrovej, Bornholm
- **Context:** unknown
- **Object:** type fragmentary; bronze; 39 mm long, 15 mm wide, 0.5 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** conventional runes (long-branch runes, bind-runes)
- **Layout:** two sides, divided into rows; two rows on each side
- **Reading order:** boustrophedon
- **Punctuation:** cross-shaped marks of punctuation
- **Decoration:** no

## Where it is now

- **Current location:** KNM: D 199/2006
- **Examined by Pereswetoff-Morath:** 2 times
