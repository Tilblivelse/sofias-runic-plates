# Leksand plate

A bronze, type A plate from a grave at Yttermo, Leksand parish, Dalarna, dated archaeologically to 1000–1100s (lV).

The book proposes a reading of the text.

## Quick facts

- **Catalogue no.:** 16
- **SRD:** D Fv1984;250
- **Other signature:** none listed
- **Found:** 1983, Yttermo, Leksand parish, Dalarna
- **Context:** grave
- **Object:** type A; bronze; 39-41 mm long, 15-17 mm wide, 0.5 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** conventional runes
- **Layout:** two sides, not divided into rows
- **Reading order:** straightforward
- **Punctuation:** no
- **Decoration:** no

## Where it is now

- **Current location:** DM: 20152:216
- **Examined by Pereswetoff-Morath:** once
