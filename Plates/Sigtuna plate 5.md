# Sigtuna plate 5

Sigtuna plate 5 has no recorded findplace beyond the general 'Black Earth' deposit and no clear context; even its Viking-Age dating is uncertain. It is copper, provisionally type B.

The inscription remains without a proposed interpretation in the book.
