# Sigtuna plate 4

Another fragmentary plate from Sigtuna, this one from the 'Granhäcken' block, again recorded as both copper and bronze; archaeological evidence dates it to 1000–1150 (lV).

No reading of the inscription is proposed in the book.

## Quick facts

- **Catalogue no.:** 27
- **SRD:** U Fv1986;220B
- **Other signature:** Sl 13
- **Found:** 1985, in the ‘Granhäcken’ block, Sigtuna, Uppland
- **Context:** domestic (urban environment)
- **Object:** type fragmentary; recorded as both copper and bronze; 45 mm long, 16-20 mm wide, 0.5 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** conventional runes (long-branch runes)
- **Layout:** two sides
- **Reading order:** undetermined
- **Punctuation:** no
- **Decoration:** no
