# Vassunda plate 3

Copper, type B, from a Christian grave at Vassunda rectory, Uppland; the runes place it only broadly in the Viking Age (V).

Sofia proposes an interpretation of the inscription.

## Quick facts

- **Catalogue no.:** 32
- **SRD:** U AST1;183
- **Other signature:** none listed
- **Found:** 1925, Vassunda rectory, Uppland
- **Context:** grave (Christian)
- **Object:** type B; copper; 48 mm long, 34 mm wide, 0.5 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** conventional (long-branch runes)
- **Layout:** two sides, divided into rows; three rows on the obverse and four on the reverse
- **Reading order:** boustrophedon
- **Punctuation:** no
- **Decoration:** no

## Where it is now

- **Current location:** Museum of Nordic Archaeology in Uppsala: 5711:3
- **Examined by Pereswetoff-Morath:** 2 times
