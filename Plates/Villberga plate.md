# Villberga plate

The Villberga plate's find circumstances are largely unrecorded: it was purchased by the Museum of Nordic Archaeology in Uppsala in 1928 with no documented findplace beyond 'Villberga, Uppland'. It is copper, type A, dated broadly to the Viking Age (V).

The book includes an interpretation of the inscription.

## Quick facts

- **Catalogue no.:** 33
- **SRD:** U AST1;174
- **Other signature:** none listed
- **Found:** unknown, purchased by Museum of Nordic Archaeology in Uppsala in 1928, unknown, Villberga, Uppland
- **Context:** unknown
- **Object:** type A; copper; 46 mm long, 32-33 mm wide, 1 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** conventional (long-branch runes), R for vowel
- **Layout:** two sides, divided into rows; three rows on each side
- **Reading order:** straightforward
- **Punctuation:** a cross-shaped mark of punctuation
- **Decoration:** no

## Where it is now

- **Current location:** Museum of Nordic Archaeology in Uppsala: 5506:28
- **Examined by Pereswetoff-Morath:** 2 times
