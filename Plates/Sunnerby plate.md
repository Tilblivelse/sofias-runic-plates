# Sunnerby plate

A fragmentary copper, type A plate from a domestic site at Sunnerby, Kållandsö, Västergötland, dated broadly to the Viking Age (V).

No reading of the inscription is proposed in the book.

## Quick facts

- **Catalogue no.:** 36
- **SRD:** no signature
- **Other signature:** none listed
- **Found:** 2012, Sunnerby, Otterstad parish on Kållandsö, Västergö tland (Sunnerby 9:1, site RAÄ 57)
- **Context:** domestic
- **Object:** type A, fragmentary; copper; 23 mm long, 22-23 mm wide, 0.8 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** conventional and unconventional runes (balanced runes)
- **Layout:** two sides; two rows on the reverse
- **Reading order:** Not recorded
- **Punctuation:** no
- **Decoration:** yes

## Where it is now

- **Current location:** Väner Museum, find no 299
- **Examined by Pereswetoff-Morath:** no
