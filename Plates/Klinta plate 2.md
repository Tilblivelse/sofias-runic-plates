# Klinta plate 2

The second of the two Klinta plates, from the same grave, sharing its copper, type A classification and 900s (eV) archaeological date.

The book does not offer an interpretation of the inscription.

## Quick facts

- **Catalogue no.:** 39
- **SRD:** Öl BN84
- **Other signature:** none listed
- **Found:** 1957, Klinta, Köpingsvik parish, Öland
- **Context:** grave
- **Object:** type A; copper; 44-45 mm long, 9-10 mm wide, 0.5 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** conventional (long-branch runes, short-twig runes)
- **Layout:** two sides, not divided into rows
- **Reading order:** straightforward
- **Punctuation:** vertical stroke as a mark of punctuation?
