# Sigtuna plate 3

A fragmentary plate from the 'Humlegården' block, Sigtuna, recorded inconsistently as both copper and bronze, assigned only broadly to the Viking Age (V).

Sofia does not attempt a reading of the inscription.

## Quick facts

- **Catalogue no.:** 26
- **SRD:** U AST1;171
- **Other signature:** Sl 4
- **Found:** 1927, in the ‘Humlegården’ block, Sigtuna, Uppland
- **Context:** domestic (urban environment)
- **Object:** type fragmentary; recorded as both copper and bronze; 42 mm long, 23 mm wide, 0.5 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** unconventional runes
- **Layout:** lines of carving on two sides
- **Reading order:** undetermined
- **Punctuation:** unclear
- **Decoration:** yes

## Where it is now

- **Current location:** SF 1305:1
- **Examined by Pereswetoff-Morath:** 2 times
