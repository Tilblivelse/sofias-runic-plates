# Solberga plate 4

A fourth plate from the Solberga 4:8 site, this one bronze, type A with wire, likewise dated broadly to the Viking Age (V).

No reading of the inscription is proposed in the book.

## Quick facts

- **Catalogue no.:** 44
- **SRD:** Öl ATA423-2541-1996
- **Other signature:** none listed
- **Found:** 1990, Solberga 4:8, Köpingsvik, Öland
- **Context:** domestic (urban environment)
- **Object:** type A (+wire); bronze; 26-27 mm long, 10-11 mm wide, 0.5 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** rune-like markings
- **Layout:** lines of carving on both sides
- **Reading order:** Not recorded
- **Punctuation:** no
- **Decoration:** yes
