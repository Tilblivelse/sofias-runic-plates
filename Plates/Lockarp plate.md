# Lockarp plate

A copper, type B plate from a rural domestic site at Lockarp, Scania, dated archaeologically to 1000–1050 (mV).

The inscription remains without a proposed interpretation in the book.

## Quick facts

- **Catalogue no.:** 8
- **SRD:** DR NOR2003;20
- **Other signature:** DK no: Sk 134
- **Found:** 2002, Lockarp, Scania
- **Context:** domestic (rural environment)
- **Object:** type B; copper; 39 mm long, 14 mm wide, 1 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** conventional (perhaps one or two bind-runes)
- **Layout:** one side, not divided into rows
- **Reading order:** straightforward
- **Punctuation:** no
- **Decoration:** no

## Where it is now

- **Current location:** Museums of Malmö: 12756:19 (?)
- **Examined by Pereswetoff-Morath:** no
