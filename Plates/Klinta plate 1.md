# Klinta plate 1

One of two copper, type A plates from a grave at Klinta, Köpingsvik parish, Öland, dated archaeologically to the 900s (eV).

No interpretation of the inscription is given in the book.

## Quick facts

- **Catalogue no.:** 38
- **SRD:** Öl BN83
- **Other signature:** none listed
- **Found:** 1957, Klinta, Köpingsvik parish, Öland
- **Context:** grave
- **Object:** type A; copper; 22 mm long, 8-9 mm wide, 0.5 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** conventional runes: short-twig rune n
- **Layout:** one side, not divided into rows
- **Reading order:** straightforward
- **Punctuation:** a cross-shaped mark of punctuation?
- **Decoration:** no

## Where it is now

- **Current location:** SHM 25840:59:3
- **Examined by Pereswetoff-Morath:** 2 times
