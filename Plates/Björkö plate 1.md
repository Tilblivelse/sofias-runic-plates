# Björkö plate 1

Björkö plate 1 is a bronze plate found at fort (The Garrison), Birka, Uppland. It is listed as type A (+copper wire). Archaeological evidence dates it to 900s (eV).

No interpretation is given in the book.

## Quick facts

- **Catalogue no.:** 18
- **SRD:** U NOR2002;26
- **Other signature:** none listed
- **Found:** 2002, fort (The Garrison), Birka, Uppland
- **Context:** domestic (urban environment, fortification)
- **Object:** type A (+copper wire); bronze; 33 mm long, 6-7 mm wide, 0.9 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** conventional runes (long-branch runes, short-twig runes b, o and possibly a)
- **Layout:** two sides, not divided into rows on the obverse, possibly two rows on the reverse
- **Reading order:** undetermined
- **Punctuation:** a seriffed cross as a mark of punctuation
- **Decoration:** ornamental lines

## Where it is now

- **Current location:** ARL; find no 7038; RAÄ no: 35
- **Examined by Pereswetoff-Morath:** once
