# Gamla Uppsala plate 1

A fragmentary copper-alloy plate from Gamla (Old) Uppsala, found in a domestic context and dated to 1000–1100s (lV).

Sofia proposes an interpretation of the inscription.

## Quick facts

- **Catalogue no.:** 20
- **SRD:** U ATA351-1796-2014
- **Other signature:** none listed
- **Found:** 2012, Gamla (Old) Uppsala, Uppland
- **Context:** domestic
- **Object:** type fragmentary; copper alloy; 22 mm long, 29 mm wide, 0.5 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** conventional runes (long-branch runes, short-twig runes a and n, one bind-rune ("el)
- **Layout:** two sides, three runes on each side
- **Reading order:** boustrophedon
- **Punctuation:** no
- **Decoration:** no

## Where it is now

- **Current location:** ‘Arkeologerna’ SHM; find no: 909; context: 32 4265; find unit: 32 4970
- **Examined by Pereswetoff-Morath:** once
