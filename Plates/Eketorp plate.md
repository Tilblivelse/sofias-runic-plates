# Eketorp plate

A fragmentary bronze plate from Eketorp Fort on Öland, found in a fortified domestic setting and dated broadly to the Viking Age (V).

The inscription remains without a proposed interpretation in the book.

## Quick facts

- **Catalogue no.:** 37
- **SRD:** Öl ATA326-1087-2007
- **Other signature:** none listed
- **Found:** 1970, Eketorp Fort, Eketorp, Gräsgård parish, Öland
- **Context:** domestic (urban, fortification)
- **Object:** type fragmentary; bronze; 40-49 mm long, 13-15 mm wide, 0.5 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** conventional and unconventional runes (balanced runes, u bind-runes as on the Roskilde plate)
- **Layout:** two sides, not divided into rows
- **Reading order:** from right to left on the A-side and straightforward on the B-side
- **Punctuation:** no
- **Decoration:** no

## Where it is now

- **Current location:** SHM 31597
- **Examined by Pereswetoff-Morath:** 3 times
