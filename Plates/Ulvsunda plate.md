# Ulvsunda plate

The Ulvsunda plate is bronze, type B, from a grave at Ulvsunda, Uppland, thought to be that of a woman and pre-Christian; both archaeological and runological evidence date it to the 800s (eV).

The book proposes a reading of the text.

## Quick facts

- **Catalogue no.:** 31
- **SRD:** U AST1;150
- **Other signature:** none listed
- **Found:** 1939, Ulvsunda, Uppland
- **Context:** grave (pre-Christian, female’s?)
- **Object:** type B; bronze; 16.5 mm long, 9.5 mm wide, 0.3 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** conventional (short-twig runes)
- **Layout:** one side, two rows
- **Reading order:** straightforward
- **Punctuation:** perhaps two straight lines (||) as a mark of termination
- **Decoration:** no

## Where it is now

- **Current location:** SHM 22695
- **Examined by Pereswetoff-Morath:** 4 times
