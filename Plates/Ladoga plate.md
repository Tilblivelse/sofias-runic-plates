# Ladoga plate

Recovered from Varangian Street in Staraja Ladoga, this copper, type A plate carries a bronze attachment. Archaeological and runological evidence together date it to 800–950 (eV).

Sofia offers a reading of the inscription.

## Quick facts

- **Catalogue no.:** 12
- **SRD:** RU Melnikova2001;196
- **Other signature:** none listed
- **Found:** 1975, Varangian Street, Staraja Ladoga, Russia
- **Context:** domestic (urban)
- **Object:** type A (+bronze attachment); copper; 48 mm long, 14.2-18 mm wide, 0.5 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** unconventional (balanced runes, older runes) and one conventional rune (s).
- **Layout:** two sides, divided into rows; two rows on each side
- **Reading order:** straightforward
- **Punctuation:** no
- **Decoration:** no

## Where it is now

- **Current location:** Staraja Ladoga Historical-Architectural and Archaeological Museum: САЕ-75. ЛП-1/1303
- **Examined by Pereswetoff-Morath:** 5 times
