# Roskilde plate

The Roskilde plate, a bronze type A plate with copper wire, was found in Roskilde itself; nothing is recorded about its find context. Runologically it is dated to 1050–1100 (lV).

An interpretation of the text is given in the book.

## Quick facts

- **Catalogue no.:** 5
- **SRD:** DR 246
- **Other signature:** DK no: Sj 27
- **Found:** 1866, Roskilde
- **Context:** unknown
- **Object:** type A (+copper wire); bronze; 90 mm long, 10-12 mm wide, 1.5 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** conventional and unconventional runes
- **Layout:** two sides, not divided into rows
- **Reading order:** straightforward
- **Punctuation:** a cross-shaped mark (x) of punctuation and a stroke (')
- **Decoration:** no

## Where it is now

- **Current location:** KNM: D 29
- **Examined by Pereswetoff-Morath:** once
