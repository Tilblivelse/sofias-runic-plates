# Solberga plate 3

A third Solberga plate, copper and type A, found nearby at Solberga 4:8 and dated only broadly to the Viking Age (V).

Sofia does not attempt a reading of the inscription.

## Quick facts

- **Catalogue no.:** 43
- **SRD:** Öl ATA6371/83
- **Other signature:** none listed
- **Found:** 1983, Solberga 4:8, Köpingsvik, Öland
- **Context:** domestic (urban environment)
- **Object:** type A; copper; 44 mm long, 26 mm wide, 1 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** unconventional runes, palimpsest?
- **Layout:** two sides, divided into rows; two rows on the obverse and three on the reverse
- **Reading order:** Not recorded
- **Punctuation:** unclear
- **Decoration:** no

## Where it is now

- **Current location:** County Museum of Kalmar?, registration number 6371/83
- **Examined by Pereswetoff-Morath:** once
