# Sigtuna plate 7

The most recently found of the seven, discovered in 2014 near 'Göte's petrol station' in Sigtuna: a copper-alloy, type B plate dated archaeologically to 1050–1150 (lV).

The book does not offer an interpretation of the inscription.

## Quick facts

- **Catalogue no.:** 30
- **SRD:** no signature
- **Other signature:** Sl 120
- **Found:** 2014, ‘Göte’s petrol station’, Sigtuna
- **Context:** domestic (urban environment)
- **Object:** type B; copper alloy; 25 mm long, 22 mm wide, 0.8 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** conventional and unconventional (bind-runes, runiforms)
- **Layout:** two sides
- **Reading order:** undetermined
- **Punctuation:** no
- **Decoration:** no
